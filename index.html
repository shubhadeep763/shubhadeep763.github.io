About

I'm an integrated PhD candidate in physics at the International Centre for Theoretical Sciences (ICTS-TIFR), Bengaluru, working in non-equilibrium statistical mechanics.

My research connects microscopic dynamics to the macroscopic laws that emerge from it — hydrodynamics and transport in classical and quantum many-body systems, active matter, and stochastic processes. I work at the interface of analytical methods — kinetic and hydrodynamic descriptions, macroscopic and fluctuating hydrodynamics, exact diagonalisation, and generalized hydrodynamics — and Python-based numerical and molecular dynamics simulation.

Research interests

  • Kinetic theory & hydrodynamics
  • Transport in low-dimensional & integrable systems
  • Generalized & fluctuating hydrodynamics
  • Active matter
  • Quantum many-body dynamics
  • Stochastic processes

Research

Jamming of persistent active particles in 1D

2025 – present

Exact steady-state cluster statistics via a mapping to constrained lattice paths, and scaling of the mean jamming time from a first-passage drift–diffusion picture, validated with event-driven molecular dynamics.

Fluctuations & transport in single-file diffusion

2025

Current and tracer cumulants for 1D diffusive systems from macroscopic fluctuation theory, with transport-coefficient maps connecting Brownian particles, hard rods, and exclusion processes.

Spin transport in the Heisenberg chain

2024

Quantum response functions computed analytically and by exact diagonalisation, and KPZ universality in spin transport studied through generalized hydrodynamics and the thermodynamic Bethe ansatz.

Quantum walks & quantum algorithms

2025

Continuous- and discrete-time quantum walks, query complexity for NAND-tree evaluation, and quantum speedup on welded-tree and hierarchical graphs simulated in Qiskit.
